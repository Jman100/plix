Getting Started

How to Install Plix

Installing on Windows

Windows 10 / 11 (x86_64 & ARM64)

  1. 1
    Download the unified plix_windows.exe installer.
  2. 2
    Run setup. It automatically selects and installs the native build for your CPU architecture (Intel/AMD or Qualcomm ARM).
  3. 3
    Launch Plix and sign in to Plex or Jellyfin.

Installing on Android / TV

Google TV • Fire TV • Android Mobile

  1. 1
    On your TV or streaming device, open the Downloader app (or your web browser).
  2. 2
    Enter quick code 7850497 into Downloader, or download plix_android.apk directly.
  3. 3
    Click Install. Sign in effortlessly using Quick Connect or Plex TV link code.

Installing on Linux

Ubuntu • Debian • Arch • Portable

  1. 1
    Ubuntu/Debian: Download plix_linux.deb and run sudo apt install ./plix_linux.deb.
  2. 2
    Arch Linux: Download plix_linux_arch.pkg.tar.zst and install with sudo pacman -U ./plix_linux_arch.pkg.tar.zst.
  3. 3
    Generic / Portable: Extract plix_linux_portable.tar.gz and launch with ./run.sh.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use both Plex and Jellyfin servers at the same time? ▼

Yes! Plix was built specifically to accommodate multi-server setups. You can add any number of Plex and Jellyfin servers, pin your preferred favorites to the top, and switch between servers with just two clicks or D-Pad presses on your remote.

Can I install Plix on my Android TV, Google TV, or Fire TV stick? ▼

Yes! Plix is packaged as a universal Android APK designed specifically for television remotes and living-room displays (including Chromecast with Google TV, Fire TV, NVIDIA Shield, and Onn streaming boxes). The fastest way to install on your TV is via the free Downloader app by entering code 7850497, or sideloading via ADB. It boots straight into a TV remote-friendly D-Pad interface with automatic row navigation and custom launcher banner support.

How does Watchlist and Continue Watching synchronization work across different servers? ▼

Plix continuously aggregates your ongoing media across all connected Plex and Jellyfin servers into a single chronological Continue Watching row. Whenever you resume or pause a movie or episode on any server, your playback progress is updated in real time and automatically synced back to that server. Watchlist additions also bookmark titles directly on the originating host.

What is Direct Play and why does Plix prefer it over transcoding? ▼

Traditional clients often force your server to transcode video and audio into lower bitrates, causing CPU overload, buffering, and washed-out HDR colors. Plix embeds a high-performance libmpv video engine with full hardware acceleration, allowing your device to play 4K HDR10, HEVC, AV1, and TrueHD/DTS audio untouched via pure Direct Play or Direct Stream.

How do I customize themes, accent colors, and ambient theme music? ▼

Head into Settings > Atmosphere to choose from 16+ curated aesthetic themes (Cyberpunk, Midnight Violet, Obsidian, Cinema Gold, Sunset Blaze, and more) or design your own custom accent palette. Plix also supports background TV show theme songs during library browsing, Picture-in-Picture on mobile, and smooth backdrop dimming.

How do application updates work? ▼

Plix includes an integrated automatic update checker that reads the official release manifest in the background. When a new version is released, you will be prompted with full release notes and a one-click download & install button directly within the app.

Does Plix require a paid Plex Pass or Jellyfin subscription? ▼

No. Plix is completely free and works with standard, free Plex accounts and open-source Jellyfin servers. There are no paywalls, subscriptions, tracking, or advertisements.

What video formats and audio pass-through are supported? ▼

Because Plix uses a native libmpv engine, it supports direct play for virtually every modern container and codec: MKV, MP4, WebM, HEVC/H.265, H.264, VP9, AV1, Dolby Vision, HDR10, and HDR10+. It also supports TrueHD, DTS-HD MA, EAC3, AC3, Opus, and FLAC multi-channel audio pass-through.
