Installing on Windows

Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)

  1. 1
    Click the Download for Windows button above to download plix_windows.exe.
  2. 2
    Run the installer. It installs per-user directly into your local app directory without requiring administrator permissions.
  3. 3

Installing on Android TV / Fire TV

Google TV • Fire OS • NVIDIA Shield

  1. 1

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use both Plex and Jellyfin servers at the same time? ▼

Yes! Plix was built specifically to accommodate multi-server setups. You can add any number of Plex and Jellyfin servers, pin your preferred favorites to the top, and switch between servers with just two clicks or D-Pad presses on your remote.

How do application updates work? ▼

Plix includes an integrated automatic update checker that reads the official release manifest in the background. When a new version is released, you will be prompted with full release notes and a one-click download & install button directly within the app.

Does Plix require a paid Plex Pass or Jellyfin subscription? ▼

No. Plix is completely free and works with standard, free Plex accounts and open-source Jellyfin servers. There are no paywalls, subscriptions, tracking, or advertisements.

What video formats and audio pass-through are supported? ▼

Because Plix uses a native libmpv engine, it supports direct play for virtually every modern container and codec: MKV, MP4, WebM, HEVC/H.265, H.264, VP9, AV1, Dolby Vision, HDR10, and HDR10+. It also supports TrueHD, DTS-HD MA, EAC3, AC3, Opus, and FLAC multi-channel audio pass-through.
